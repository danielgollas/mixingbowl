# PROJECT BRIEF: AGGRESSIVE HIGH-VOLUME VEGAN DIET PROGRAM

### 1. EXECUTION OVERVIEW & STRATEGY
* **User Profile:** Heavy volume eater, experiences peak cravings late at night.
* **Core Strategy:** **The Reverse Pyramid Protocol.** Calorie-restricted, high-protein intake during daylight hours to preserve a massive calorie budget for high-volume, low-calorie eating in the late evening/night.
* **Core Protein Anchor:** Tofu (Firm & Silken) combined with non-starchy vegetable volume padding.
* **Primary Cooking Modalities:** Air Fryer & Stovetop.

### 2. MACRONUTRIENT & METRIC TARGETS
* **Daily Calorie Ceiling:** 1,200 – 1,500 kcal
* **Target Weight Loss Velocity:** ~1.5 to 2 lbs per week (Projected Timeline: 10–12 weeks for 20 lbs)
* **Protein Target:** 0.8 to 1.0g per pound of target body weight.
* **Health Safeguard Note:** Strictly reject mono-diet structures (e.g., all-tofu or all-potato) to defend kidney health against nitrogen strain, preserve gastrointestinal motility via high fiber, and prevent micronutrient deficiencies (Vitamin C, B12, A).

### 4. MASTER RECIPE SPECIFICATIONS & INDEX

#### Recipe 1: The Infinite Tofu Scramble
* **Ingredients:** 150g firm tofu, 2 cups spinach, 1 cup sliced mushrooms, 1 cup riced cauliflower, 1 tbsp nutritional yeast, garlic powder, turmeric, salt.
* **Execution:** Mash tofu into a stovetop pan. Add riced cauliflower immediately (acts as a volume multiplier, absorbing moisture and seasonings). Fold in mushrooms and spinach at the end until wilted.

#### Recipe 2: Crispy Air-Fried Tofu Base
* **Ingredients:** 150g extra-firm tofu (pressed), soy sauce, smoked paprika, garlic powder.
* **Execution:** Cube tofu, toss in seasonings. Air fry at 400°F (200°C) for 15-20 minutes until crisp.

#### Recipe 3: The Massive Blended Protein Fluff
* **Ingredients:** 150g silken tofu, 1 cup frozen strawberries/blueberries, splash of unsweetened almond milk, 1/4 tsp xanthan gum, stevia.
* **Execution:** Blend on high for 3 full minutes. The xanthan gum forces air into the silken tofu base, expanding the mixture into a massive dessert bowl texture.

#### Recipe 4: Late-Night Charred Cabbage Chips
* **Ingredients:** 1 head green cabbage, olive oil spray, garlic salt, onion powder, nutritional yeast.
* **Execution:** Slice into thick wedges or steaks. Spray lightly with oil. Coat heavily with dry spices. Bake at 400°F or air fry until edges are deeply caramelized and crispy.

### 5. IMPLEMENTATION SAUCE MODULE (LOW-CALORIE FLAVOR AGENTS)
1. **The Spicy Creamy Mayo (~20 kcal):** Whisk 1/4 cup silken tofu, 1 tbsp sriracha, splash of apple cider vinegar, garlic powder, pinch of salt.
2. **Sweet & Savory Garlic Soy Glaze (~15 kcal):** Simmer 3 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce, 1 tbsp rice vinegar, 1 minced garlic clove, 1/2 tsp ginger, 2 tsp granulated stevia.
3. **Zesty Cheesy Garlic Dressing (~35 kcal):** Vigorously stir 2 tbsp nutritional yeast, 2 tbsp warm water, 1 tbsp lemon juice, 1/2 tsp garlic powder, salt, pepper.
4. **Zero-Calorie Tangy Buffalo Wing Sauce (~3 kcal):** Whisk cayenne hot sauce, white vinegar, garlic salt, pinch of xantham gum for thickness.
5. **Sticky Sweet Sesame-Free Glaze (~8 kcal):** Soy sauce, rice vinegar, monk fruit sweetener, thickened with a tiny pinch of xanthan gum.
6. **Creamy Dill Pickle Dip (~18 kcal):** Blend 1/4 cup silken tofu, 2 tbsp pickle juice, 1 tbsp finely diced dill pickles, dried dill.
7. **Smokey Maple-BBQ Reduction (~25 kcal):** Tomato paste base, liquid smoke, apple cider vinegar, maple-flavored stevia drops, onion powder.
8. **Creamy Lemon-Herb "Tahini" Swap (~16 kcal):** Silken tofu, heavy lemon juice, garlic paste, dried oregano, parsley, thinned with warm water.
